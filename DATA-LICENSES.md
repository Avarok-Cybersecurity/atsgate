# Data licenses

ATSGate's code is dual-licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0. Data files that ATSGate will ship (taxonomy
alias tables, built-in blueprints derived from occupational data) are **separate works** with their
own licenses, kept in their own files with their own attribution and version.

**No third-party data is imported yet.** This file states the terms the data will ship under, so
the credit lines are settled before the first import (phase 3).

require.

## ESCO (planned: ESCO v1.2.1)

ESCO is published by the European Commission and downloadable free of charge from
<https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/use-esco/download>. That page states no licence of its own; it links
the Commission's [legal notice](https://commission.europa.eu/legal-notice_en) (opened
2026-11-20), under which EU-owned content is reused under CC BY 4.0 per the Commission Decision of
12 December 2011 on the reuse of Commission documents (CELEX 32011D0833), provided appropriate credit
is given and changes are indicated. Every distribution of ESCO-derived data will carry:

> This product uses the ESCO classification of the European Commission (ESCO v1.2.1), reused under
> the Commission's reuse policy (Decision 2011/833/EU, CC BY 4.0). Avarok Cybersecurity has modified
> it by selecting labels and building alias tables. The European Commission does not endorse this
> product.

ESCO trade marks and logos are excluded from the reuse policy and are not used.

## Not used

- **Lightcast Open Skills**: its terms depend on the licence requested and are not stated openly
  ([lightcast-faq](CITATIONS.md)), so its data is not embedded.

## Test fixtures

The resumes under `fixtures/` describe an invented person and are generated by this repository's
own code (`cargo run -p xtask -- fixtures --write`). They are covered by the code license.

The render inputs under `fixtures/career/`, `fixtures/tailoring/` and `fixtures/contact/` describe
an invented person and are covered by the code license. `fixtures/import/tbraun96-career.json` is a
copy of the career file of ATSGate's author, Thomas Braun (no phone number or street address), included
at his request as a real-world import test; `fixtures/import/tbraun96.career.json` is generated
from it.

## Fonts

`atsgate-render` embeds Libertinus Serif Regular and Bold
([`crates/atsgate-render/fonts/`](crates/atsgate-render/fonts)), copyright 2012-2024 The Libertinus
Project Authors, under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](crates/atsgate-render/fonts/OFL.txt). The
font files are unmodified and ship with that license; the OFL does not apply to the resumes
rendered with them. No other font is embedded: `typst-assets`' `fonts` feature is not enabled,
because it would also bundle a GPL-3.0 (with font exception) font.
